# One seat, one take

1. A humanoid waves from the lounge floor; two friends sit behind.
2. The same character turns and walks around the coffee table.
3. They stop, take the middle cushion and face the viewer.
4. The camera pulls back to all three friends; VELNEST's name closes the piece.

Sources: current GLBs, Wave/Walk/Sit clips, sofa coordinates and shared pathfinding. Placements are staged; no participant counts or verified-identity claims.
