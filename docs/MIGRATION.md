## What was recoverable verbatim

The source conversation exposed the following user decisions and requirements:

1. Choose approach B: subscribers log into an independent website with Twitch, select a character, control it by keyboard or phone, and OBS shows the result live.
2. Build a small world inspired by the social presence of VRChat and Roblox, but at hackathon scope.
3. Start with three character choices.
4. Let a streamer eventually customize characters and parts of the world, within moderation rules.
5. Let subscriber milestones unlock more character slots.
6. Let subscriber/fan level unlock avatar items; Channel Points may also unlock items without payment.
7. Keep the interactive space subscriber-only to make the community feel intentional rather than crowded.
8. Reassess name uniqueness, time feasibility, token/cost feasibility, difficult manual work, and video-production needs.
9. Reduce completion expectations to match the remaining hackathon time.
10. Continue the work on the desktop after returning home.
