# VELNEST demonstration credits

## Original project and music

VELNEST's room assembly scripts, humanoid characters, animation, application and
video layout were developed for this entry with AI-assisted tools. The background
score is synthesized from oscillators and noise by `video-2026/music/compose.py`;
the inspected generator imports no third-party music samples or loops.

## Furniture

Four Poly Haven assets are used under CC0-1.0. Exact source records, authors and
checksums are in [the furniture credits](../art/vendor/polyhaven/LICENSE.md).
Licence: https://polyhaven.com/license

## Sound effects

The film uses HyperFrames' bundled Pixabay sound-effect library under the
Pixabay Content License: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/

The ten local files (chime, click-soft, impact-bass-1, pop, riser, sparkle,
typing, whoosh-cinematic, whoosh-short and whoosh) were compared by SHA-256
against the installed library and all matched. Its supplied CREDITS.md identifies
the source and permits derivative videos. These sounds are not claimed as CC0
or as original compositions.

## Narration and fonts

The final 3-minute film uses locally synthesized English narration, Kokoro
`am_puck`, rather than the earlier Windows Zira guide tracks. The official model
card identifies Apache-2.0 licensed weights:
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M

Manrope and Inter are used under the SIL Open Font License:
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt
