# VELNEST QA — October 29, 2026

Story: a Twitch viewer selects a character and accessory, enters the shared
room, moves/reacts/reserves a seat, and appears in the broadcaster's OBS room.

## Findings resolved

1. **P2 — room texture cleanup:** `dispose3D` omitted texture objects. The new
   textured lounge made this significant when entering/leaving repeatedly.
   Cleanup now disposes each unique material texture exactly once; a regression
   test covers shared maps, repeated materials and repeated geometry.
2. **P2 — stale motion-study pipeline:** the film bundled the old room;
   rebuilding with the compressed lounge failed without Draco. Its build now
   copies the current room and local decoder, uses the website's lighting, and
   supplies a valid URL base for Three.js inside the classic-script bundle.
   HyperFrames runtime, layout, contrast and craft checks pass.
3. **P2 — fragmented verification:** there was no single complete QA command or
   CI workflow. `pnpm qa` now typechecks, builds and runs eleven suites in order.
   The workflow covers Windows/Linux, has read-only repository permissions,
   uses the pinned package manager and frozen lockfile, and needs no Twitch keys.
4. **P3 — obsolete seat language:** controls still referred to sofa cushions.
   Labels now describe the visible lounge chairs; event IDs and coordinates
   are preserved.

The refreshed motion study is verified at 1920×1080, 30 fps, 360 frames and
12 seconds. Its first multi-worker export had white WebGL frames and was
rejected. The single-worker replacement decoded fully, has twelve distinct
sample frames, and its contact sheet was visually inspected. It is staged
