# VELNEST

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Twitch streamers and their communities share an interactive 3D lounge.
Signed-in viewers choose a humanoid character, move, react and sit together.
The streamer displays the shared room through an OBS Browser Source and can
manage participants. Anonymous viewers can watch. The owner explicitly chose
open admission rather than requiring subscriptions.

## Capabilities and constraints

Three modular characters: Riff, Arc and Muse. Six authored motion clips and
three interchangeable accessories. Server-authoritative movement, pathfinding,
three reserved seats, 24-avatar capacity and broadcaster-only room moderation.
Twitch credentials remain server-side. Demo mode is explicitly labeled.
All downloads and project artifacts are kept on D:.

## Brand commitments

VELNEST is all-ages, gaming-community focused, humanoid rather than furry.
The owner rejected the first simple room and chose an esports night lounge:
dark materials, purple/cyan neon and gaming equipment. Existing interface
controls and character identity should remain recognizable.

## Evidence and open work

Real Twitch broadcaster authorization and local OBS synchronization were
verified. A local rehearsal exists. Public hosting, a second real viewer and
the final Twitch/YouTube contest demonstration are not yet established.
Third-party room furniture has CC0 source and checksum records.

## Accessibility

Honor reduced motion, retain keyboard controls and provide an accessible
participant roster even when visual nameplates are crowded.
